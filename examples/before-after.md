# Before / after

## Marketing → plain

**Before**

> Our cutting-edge platform delivers a seamless, robust, and effortless
> experience that leverages AI to drive world-class outcomes.

**After**

> This platform uses AI to improve task outcomes.

## Slop → simple

**Before**

> Utilize the lever to initiate the procedure. The system will leverage the
> data to perform an analysis of the results.

**After**

> Use the lever to start the procedure. The system uses the data to analyze
> the results.

## Long sentence → split

**Before**

> The technician should verify that the pressure is within limits, that the
> temperature is stable, and that no leaks are present before starting the
> engine, which is the most critical step in the whole procedure.

**After**

> Verify that the pressure is within limits. Verify that the temperature is
> stable. Verify that no leaks are present. Then start the engine.

## Passive → active

**Before**

> The valve was closed by the operator after the warning was received.

**After**

> The operator closed the valve after the team sent the warning.

## Semicolons → separate sentences

**Before**

> Check the pressure; then open the valve.

**After**

> Check the pressure. Then open the valve.
